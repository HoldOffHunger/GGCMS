# EarthFluent

Working notes for the next phase. Written 1 September 2026, at the end of a
long session, so the next one starts from knowledge rather than rediscovery.

## What it is

A language-learning site on GGCMS. **6,556 distinct entry titles**, vocabulary
quizzes, pronunciation checking through the browser's speech API, and a
dictionary of 113,609 definitions shared across every site in the estate.

It is not a prototype. The database, the quiz logic, the lesson structure, the
pronunciation matching and the content are all built and all working. What it
has never had is a designer.

## The idea worth building around

The header runs a slideshow of **famous images from the culture of the language
being learned**, and the vocabulary quiz illustrates each word with a live
image search rather than a translation.

Both come from the same instinct, and it is a good one: *a word should attach
to the thing, not to another word.* You did not learn your first language by
being told that "dog" means anything. That instinct is the product's actual
differentiator and nothing about the current design foregrounds it.

**Outstanding**

## The remodel

The brief, in the author's words: *the function remains, but everything looks
like something else entirely.* A remodelled bathroom. Sleek, business-like,
the polish of a well-funded competitor.

### The one thing to get right

**Do not build toward DuoLingo.** Its visual language — flat, rounded, cartoon
mascot, gamified streaks — is the house style of the whole category, and
competing inside it means being a smaller version of the market leader.

EarthFluent has something structural that DuoLingo does not: **cultural
imagery, and a reason for it to be there.** The design that follows from that
is editorial rather than app-like — image-forward, generous, closer to a
well-made magazine than a game. Same polish, different register, and it is a
register nobody in the category occupies.

### What white backgrounds are and are not

The white background is probably not the problem. Redesigns that fail usually
fail on **spacing, type scale and hierarchy**, and those read as "amateur" far
more loudly than colour does. A dark or tinted palette is a legitimate choice;
disciplined typography is a requirement either way. Fix the second first and
the first becomes a preference rather than a rescue.

### What can be made here, and what cannot

Layout systems, type scales, spacing rules, colour palettes, CSS, SVG marks,
icons, diagrams and component design: yes. **Photographs: no.** The cultural
slideshow images have to come from somewhere with a licence attached, and that
is a sourcing job, not a drawing one.

Two constraints inherited from the engine: stylesheets are generated by a
format class rather than written as files ([PageCache.md](../Docs/PageCache.md)),
and per-site presentation lives in
`usr/lib/ggcms/src/templates/earthfluent/`, which is moving to the private
estate repository.

## Sequencing

**Bug-hunt first, then remodel.** The author's own call and the right one — a
new façade over broken plumbing is a worse product than plain plumbing that
works, and every fault found afterwards gets blamed on the redesign.

The hunt is cheap now in a way it was not yesterday: the ISE queue is counted
tickets rather than a journal, so *what is actually broken on earthfluent, ranked
by how often it fires* is one query.

```sql
SELECT id, IncidentCount, Script, LEFT(ErrorMessage,90), LastModificationDate
FROM earthfluent.InternalServerError ORDER BY IncidentCount DESC LIMIT 20;
```

Then the remodel, then decide about ads — the site is free today and the author
may want to monetise it later, which is a reason to build a design that can
carry advertising gracefully rather than one that has to be torn up to make room.
